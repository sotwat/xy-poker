## Diagnosis and rejected experiments

On the same 20 post-hoc midgame states from the first 10 deals of the 10,000-game
evaluation, the 8,000 / 10,000 models predict mean utility +0.3772 / +0.4182,
while every observed terminal utility is -1. This is evidence of optimistic
predictions on these trajectories, not a causal explanation of all losses.
The large Y deficit persists. Shallow 2–3-ply early search and completion-world
bias remain unresolved; more training games alone have not produced wins.

At the saved 10,064-game state, the 20,000 replay records contain:

- 2,854 A9-actor targets: 2,823 winning, 31 losing labels.
- 2,860 learner-advice targets: 31 winning, 2,829 losing labels.
- 14,286 search targets.

The **conditional trajectory distribution**, rather than the overall class
balance, is skewed: expert data comes from A9 playing the weak learner. Expert
versus expert continuations are absent. This motivates testing a broader
teacher distribution; it does not establish that this skew caused the losses.

Two frozen-copy 32-step experiments were rejected. Fitting policy only to
teacher targets worsened the diagnostic teacher cross-entropies from the
uniform-update 2.654 / 2.484 to 2.702 / 2.499. Random suit augmentation worsened
them to 2.836 / 2.705 and learner-advice value MSE from 0.440 to 0.900. No live
weights, optimizer, replay or RNG were changed by these experiments.
The first 15,000 records were used for extra updates and the last 5,000 for
measurement; all records had already been available to prior training. This
is a correlated replay diagnostic, **not independent generalization or strength
validation**. Seed100524101 and augmentation seed100524201 are recorded in
`gto_learning_objective_diagnostic_20261024.json`; teacher-only sampling also
changes subsequent minibatch RNG consumption. No confirming match sample was
spent on these failed candidates.

## Learning revision

`mixed-a9-expert-pairs-worlds8-v3` changes the eight-game cycle to self2,
A9-versus-A9 teacher2, learner-versus-A9 with advice2, past-model2. Each expert
game records both acting players' legal A9 one-hot actions and **their actual
seat-relative terminal returns**, without duplicating value records or
pretending those are learned-policy wins. Learned-policy evaluation rejects
the teacher-only opponent mode. Learned actions in advice games still come
from the learner's search. Search budget, terminal win objective, architecture,
learning rate and replay capacity are unchanged. Old replay is not relabelled;
ordinary bounded-buffer replacement continues.

This is an experimental imitation curriculum, not a demonstrated GTO or
production-strength improvement. The new A9/A9 data-path check uses a fresh
seat-swapped seed100524301 (one pair / two games), separately from the 50-deal
strength comparison. It is not a 100-game A9/A9 adoption control.
The actual trial produced 62 legal samples with 31 positive and 31 negative
seat-relative labels. The frozen network mean value was +0.0913 versus mean
return 0. This single expert pair does not by itself demonstrate a calibration
failure or improvement. The trial did not modify the live replay or weights.
