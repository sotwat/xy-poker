## Debug follow-up (2026-10-23)

The result's lettering now uses each PNG's actual aspect ratio at one cap height. Previously the fallback text width controlled the mask fit, making narrow 1s much taller than other digits. Result names, bonus labels and hand details use the selected Vectura renderer; footer labels fit the same three columns. Rating history wraps between the old and new values at narrow widths. Changing the system motion preference now explicitly restores final scores; error results hide the unfinished SKIP control.

AI deals are retained by authenticated user and token across Socket.IO reconnects (four recent deals per player, two-hour TTL, 2,000 overall). The existing 30-second minimum remains: verified early records return a finite wait and the client retries once. Delayed callbacks are ignored after a new match starts. Signed-in AI matches require a tracked deal before play; failure stays on the lobby with a retryable error. Reconnect re-submits the current pending AI result. Records no longer blindly upload historical local games without their required token. Local replays are retained. Online record acknowledgments remain separate from their reward receipt event.

Limits: this bounded deal store is process memory, so a backend restart still invalidates an unfinished AI deal. Historical untracked results are not retroactively awarded. Reward amounts and tactical AI were not changed.

Follow-up verification: 132 tests passed, lint/TypeScript/production build passed. BrowserOS inspected 320×568, 390×844 and desktop; synthetic 11/15 result, five-digit reward, error state and live reduced-motion switching; a normal guest AI match through rules, showdown, result, reward receipt and hand breakdown; signed-in Records/replay start/end, rules tabs and private-room create/cancel. The public signed-in reward flow is checked after deployment. Two-player online completion and a process restart during play were not exercised in this pass.
